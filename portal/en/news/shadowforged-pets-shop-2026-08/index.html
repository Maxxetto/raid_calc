New Shadowforged pets available in the in-game shop

Five Shadowforged pets have been spotted in the in-game shop and are being added to the Pet Compendium.

Pets added

Pet screenshot Details
`[S73SF] Cerbom` is a Spirit/Earth pet. Main skills: Special Regeneration, Vampiric Attack and Ready to Crit.
`[S74SF] Nibbles` is an Earth/Air pet. Main skills: Elemental Weakness, Durable Rock Shield and Shadow Slash.
`[S75SF] Saizeek` is an Air/Water pet. Main skills: Revenge Strike, Shadow Slash and Soul Burn.
`[S76SF] Unturex` is a Water/Fire pet. Main skills: Death Blow, Vampiric Attack and infinite Special Regeneration.
`[S77SF] Ovidox` is a Fire/Spirit pet. Main skills: Elemental Weakness, Revenge Strike and Ready to Crit.

Standout pick

Unturex is especially interesting for Water/Fire because it has infinite Special Regeneration at 70%. If you do not already have a stronger Water/Fire alternative, this pet is a very strong pickup and close to a must-have for that element pair.

There is also a Water event coming soon: Shadow Grubs Water should be available directly from that event, making it easier to tier up Unturex.
