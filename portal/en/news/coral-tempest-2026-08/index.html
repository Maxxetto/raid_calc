Coral Tempest event: schedule, currencies and rewards

The Coral Tempest event runs from August 26 to September 16, 2026. A few scheduled activities continue slightly past that date, and Limited Shop items stay purchasable until September 23.

Event schedule

  • Rare Spawn: August 26 - September 16
  • Blitz PVP Arena: August 26-28
  • Guild War: August 28-31
  • PVP Arena: August 28 - September 4
  • Blitz Raid Boss: August 31 - September 2
  • Raid Boss: September 4-6
  • PVP Arena: September 4-9
  • Blitz Guild War: September 7-8
  • Blitz PVP Arena: September 9-11
  • Guild War: September 11-14
  • PVP Arena: September 11-18
  • Blitz Raid Boss: September 14-15

Exact reward numbers per activity are tracked in the app's News tab. A community cheat sheet with the full activity/reward breakdown is also available: Open cheat sheet.

Currencies and armor

Playing during the event earns Verdant Ripple, Sunken Amber, Zephyr Crest and Abyssal Spine. Spend them in the Limited Shop to obtain the Trench Leviathan armor and upgrade it up to T7. Reaching Trench Leviathan T6 also unlocks the option to obtain Typhoon Crest T7.

Armor upgrade path

  • Buy the base Trench Leviathan armor (T1) in the Limited Shop with event shards.
  • Fuse it with Leviathan Scales and Abyssal Geodes (also in the Limited Shop) to upgrade it up to T7.
  • Once Trench Leviathan reaches T6, fuse it with Tempest Cores (Limited Shop) to also obtain the Typhoon Crest armor.
  • Remember to also pick up the matching jewelry sets from the Limited Shop to unlock the full power of both armors.
  • Reference stats shown in the event graphic: Typhoon Crest T7 around 60163/50634, Trench Leviathan T7 around 59121/49757 (full base/bonus stat breakdown to follow once available for Wargear Wardrobe).

Pets

Two new Shadowforged pets are available for event shards in the Limited Shop:

Netherp (Net, Water/Water, Tier V) carries Elemental Weakness, Revenge Strike and Special Regeneration - full numbers are on the card above. At 70% meter charge, this Special Regeneration is effectively infinite in real raid usage for Shadowforged and Epic Primal pets, which makes Netherp a strong F2P pick for Raids and Blitz Raids. Paired with Elemental Weakness, it also chips away at enemy Attack every couple of turns, a handy combo for the longer fights that infinite Special Regeneration makes possible.

See our upcoming FAQ guide on how to get the most out of infinite Special Regeneration in Raids.

Brassoc (Bra, Water/Fire, Tier V) carries Death Blow, Durable Rock Shield and Ready to Crit - full numbers are on the card above. It has no Special Regeneration, so it plays more as a straightforward crit-and-defense pet than a meter-focused one.

Tip: to get enough shards for both Netherp and its Water Shadow Grub, aim for full milestones on Blitz Raid Boss (Monday, August 31) and the weekend Raid Boss (Friday, September 4) in the same event week.

Rare Spawn

A special rare monster appears in select Epic areas and drops Verdant Ripple.

Boss Collections

Three 10-step Boss Collections run during the event. Each step is unlocked by buying the matching "Tempest Shell" item in the Limited Shop with event shards. Boss Collection rewards must be claimed before the Limited Shop closes on September 23, 2026 - buy every Tempest Shell step you want before that date.

  • The Tempest Collection (below) is available to everyone.
  • The Fortunate Collection requires the Fortunate Tempest Shell, available in the Webshop for $14.99, in addition to the regular Tempest Shells. It unlocks a different reward track with extra items.
  • The Sapphire Collection unlocks Sapphire Wings using two of the special event pets (Netherp and Brassoc) instead of a Webshop purchase.
