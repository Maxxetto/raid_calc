Season 122 Fire datamine preview

The September 2026 datamine points to Season 122 as a Fire season. The sheet is credited to Kasper534 and appears to include the season lineup, accessories, pets and several additional armor sets.

The second image appears to show the next Ultimate Armor. If the existing naming sequence continues from Ultimate Quatuordecimus and Ultimate Quintusdecimus, this would be the 16th Ultimate Armor in the line. The final in-game name is not confirmed from the image alone.

Event armor note

Some armor sets in the datamine also look like they may belong to an upcoming event. The elements shown suggest a Fire-focused event rather than the Water event many players were expecting, so it may not happen during the current August window.

As always with datamines, details can change before release.

Special thanks to Kasper534 for sharing the datamine.
