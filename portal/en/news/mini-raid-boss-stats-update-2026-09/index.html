Mini Raid Boss stats and difficulty updated

DECA has published the follow-up update to the stats and difficulty for Mini Raid Boss events (in-app "Blitz Raid Boss") promised alongside the earlier big Raid Boss update. The Blitz simulator and Boss stats reference now use the new values below. HP stayed the same at every level - only Attack and Defense changed.

Level New ATK New DEF HP Previous ATK Previous DEF

2 17,302 17,388 5,000 16,170 16,250

4 37,405 19,920 150,000 35,288 18,792
5 45,932 28,108 200,000 42,927 26,269

Note: These values are only for Mini Raid Boss (Blitz Raid Boss) events. big Raid Boss stats were updated separately - see Big Raid Boss stats and difficulty updated.
