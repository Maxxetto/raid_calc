Submit community armor and design ideas

Kasper534 has created a shared community document to collect armor, pet and visual design ideas in one place. The goal is to make community-made concepts easier to browse, share and forward to Deca when useful.

You can submit ideas that you made yourself or designs created with AI tools. The document is public to view and share, but only Kasper can add or edit entries, so anyone who wants something included should contact him first.

How to submit

1. Prepare the image or design concept you want to suggest. 2. Contact Kasper on Discord as `Kasper534`. 3. Explain whether the design is handmade, edited from existing material or AI-generated. 4. Kasper can then decide how to add it to the shared collection.
